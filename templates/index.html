How CampusSkill AI Works

Four systematic steps to transform engineering students into confident campus placement candidates.

1. Profile & Skills

Record your current technical proficiencies across Python, SQL, DSA, Web Tech, Aptitude, and Communication.

2. Target Role Matching

Select career targets such as Data Analyst, Software Engineer, or Frontend Developer to compare required vs current skill levels.

3. Gap Analysis

Our rule-based engine categorizes missing and weak competencies and computes an explainable weighted readiness score.

4. 4-Phase Roadmap

Execute a tailored preparation roadmap from core fundamentals to hands-on exercises, mini-projects, and interview prep.
